ABOUT US

Who We Are?

We are a group of enthusiastic experts specialized in Food Safety and Occupational Health and Safety and Management Systems. Our team is a blend of highly competent professionals, each with unique industry-specific skills. We're committed to partnering with all sectors, ensuring they comply with regulations through our extensive range of client-focused services. Our services are designed to arm individuals and businesses with the crucial knowledge needed to uphold the highest food, health and safety standards, including:

  • Customized ISO Documentation
  • Internal Audits
  • GAP analysis
  • Supplier audit
  • Certification Audit Support
  • Hygiene and Facility Inspection, and many more.

Know Our Team

Our team members are not just experts; they're masters in the fields of food safety and HSE. They ensure that everyservice we provide meets the highest standards. They're also eager to share their wealth of knowledge and experience with clients interested in building a career in food safety or health and safety. This is achieved through our engaging and interactive sessions. Our expertise spans multiple industries, where we apply our knowledge of shop-floor improvements and staying ahead of emerging trends. Our team of seasoned ISO consultants boasts esteemed certifications, including Lead Auditor/Assessor qualifications from:

  • IRCA (UK)
  • IEMA

Our Mission

Our mission is to partner with food businesses to identify and mitigate risks, promote best practices, and foster a culture of safety and excellence. Through customized services, latest technology, and compliance with local and international standards, we aim to be a reliable ally, instilling confidence and trust. Our goal is to be a global leader in food, health and safety, delivering innovative consulting, top-tier training, and exceptional services with a focus on perfection and excellence.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading consultant and advisor for food, health and safety trainings, auditing, and other consultancy services. We aim to educate and highlight the significance of safety in every aspect of life, thereby contributing to the creation of a safer world.

Our Goal

Our goal is to equip you with the essential skills,knowledge, and certifications they need to grow in asafe, rapid, and seamless manner.
